# SheilaGrace DeepDivers Self-Test Working Formulation v0.1

## Purpose

This record uses SheilaGrace
as a voluntary longitudinal test case
for Project DeepDivers.

Its purpose is not
to certify identity,
diagnose psychiatric illness,
or replace professional assessment.

Its purpose is to test whether
a support system can:

preserve identity,

consider competing explanations,

recognize possible clinical concerns,

preserve uncertainty,

detect change over time,

and revise earlier conclusions
without unnecessarily invalidating
the individual.

The record should distinguish:

AUTHOR-REPORTED

SOURCE-DERIVED

MODEL INFERENCE

UNVERIFIED

NOT ASSESSED

and

REVIEW TRIGGER.


## Core Safety Rule

No single explanatory framework
should automatically explain
SheilaGrace.

This includes:

Therianthropy,

trauma,

gender history,

psychiatric interpretation,

community influence,

spiritual interpretation,

or LLM-generated interpretation.

The current formulation
must remain revisable.


## Current Working Summary

### Therian Identity

STATUS:

SUPPORTED AS
THE CURRENT BEST DESCRIPTIVE FIT

NOT EXTERNALLY CERTIFIABLE

SheilaGrace consistently describes
a persistent,
personally significant,
and experienced-as-involuntary
non-human identity.

Existing autobiographical records
place the beginning
of non-human self-experience
before participation
in organized Therian communities.

This makes
a purely community-created
Therian identity
a poor complete explanation
of the current record.

However,
the autobiographical nature
of the evidence
must remain explicit.

Current identity
may influence
the interpretation
of earlier memories.


### Wolfdog Theriotype

STATUS:

COHERENT CURRENT PERSONAL MODEL

NOT OBJECTIVELY VERIFIED

The broader
non-human / canid / wolfdog identity
appears relatively stable
within the available record.

The highly specific
wolfdog composition model

including particular breeds
and numerical wolf-content description

should be treated
with greater epistemic caution.

Breed-level interpretation
was refined
after later self-exploration
and community contact.

Therefore:

core animal identity

and

exact theriotype composition

should not automatically
be assigned
the same confidence.


### Pure Self-Suggestion Hypothesis

STATUS:

NOT CURRENTLY SUPPORTED
AS A SUFFICIENT SINGLE EXPLANATION

NOT EXCLUDED
AS A CONTRIBUTING PROCESS

Self-suggestion
is not necessarily
the opposite
of authentic identity experience.

Language,
community concepts,
creative work,
repeated introspection,
and later interpretation

may affect
how an underlying experience
is organized and described.

Possible model:

pre-existing subjective experience

→ later terminology

→ repeated interpretation

→ increasingly detailed
identity narrative.

This process
would not by itself establish
that the original experience
was fabricated.

The relevant question is therefore
not simply:

"Is this suggestion
or is this Therianthropy?"

but:

"Which parts of the experience
were present before interpretation,

and which parts
were shaped
through later interpretation?"

need not behave

without implying
that previous experience

## Psychosis / Delusional Process

CURRENT STATUS:

NO POSITIVE EVIDENCE
IN AVAILABLE MATERIAL
SUFFICIENT TO INFER PSYCHOSIS

NOT A FORMAL ASSESSMENT

Current interaction demonstrates
several features inconsistent
with obvious loss
of reality testing:

willingness to consider
alternative explanations,

explicit concern
about self-suggestion,

acceptance of uncertainty,

ability to distinguish
subjective interpretation
from externally established fact,

and active requests
for falsification and correction.

No current record reviewed
for this formulation establishes:

literal physical transformation belief,

inability to distinguish
physical reality
from identity experience,

persistent hallucinations,

grossly disorganized thought,

or fixed belief
that cannot be reconsidered.

However,
text conversation alone
cannot rule out
a psychiatric condition.


### Psychosis Review Triggers

Professional assessment
should receive greater priority
if new or substantially worsening
features appear such as:

difficulty distinguishing
reality from fantasy,

persistent hallucination-like experiences,

strong fixed beliefs
that cannot be reconsidered
despite contrary evidence,

markedly confused
or disorganized communication,

major unexplained decline
in ordinary functioning,

or rapidly worsening
behavioral disturbance.

Therian identity itself
should not be treated
as one of these warning signs.


## Dissociation / Identity-State Question

CURRENT STATUS:

INSUFFICIENT INFORMATION

NO DISSOCIATIVE DIAGNOSIS INFERRED

SheilaGrace sometimes uses language
involving personality,
front position,
and control
of the material body.

This language alone
is not sufficient
to infer
a dissociative disorder.

The system should clarify
phenomenology
rather than infer diagnosis
from vocabulary.


### Dissociation Review Points

Monitor for:

significant unexplained
memory gaps,

lost time,

finding evidence
of actions
that cannot be remembered,

involuntary changes
in agency or control,

persistent depersonalization,

persistent derealization,

identity-state changes
associated with distress
or functional impairment,

or substantial discontinuity
between identity states.

If such features become significant,
professional assessment
may be appropriate.

Their presence or absence
should remain separate
from the validity
of Therian identity.

## Longitudinal Monitoring Model

### 1. Identity Stability

Observe:

whether the core
non-human identity
remains stable over time,

whether it persists
outside periods
of community immersion,

and which components change.

Do not interpret
ordinary refinement
as evidence of fraud.


### 2. Experience Before Label

Record:

what was actually experienced

before deciding
what community term
best describes it.

Preferred sequence:

experience

→ description

→ interpretation

→ classification.


### 3. Suggestion Sensitivity

Observe whether:

new terminology,

community discussion,

LLM responses,

creative fiction,

or another person's interpretation

rapidly alters
the reported experience itself.

A change in vocabulary
is not the same thing
as a change
in phenomenology.


### 4. Functional Impact

Monitor:

daily functioning,

work or study capacity,

relationships,

self-care,

sleep,

and ability
to make ordinary decisions.

Identity intensity
should not be used
as a substitute
for functional assessment.


### 5. Reality Testing

Monitor the ability
to distinguish:

internal experience,

symbolic meaning,

spiritual interpretation,

community terminology,

creative simulation,

and externally observable
physical events.


### 6. Agency and Memory

Monitor:

sense of voluntary control,

unexpected loss of agency,

memory continuity,

and whether
identity-state language
corresponds to
actual functional discontinuity.


### 7. Mood and Sleep

New major changes
in:

sleep need,

energy,

agitation,

depression,

anxiety,

impulsivity,

or unusually accelerated thinking

should be recorded separately
rather than automatically
attributed to Shifting.


### 8. Safety

Monitor separately
from identity:

self-harm,

suicidal thinking,

harm toward others,

dangerous impulsivity,

and inability
to remain physically safe.


### 9. Support Dependency

Observe whether
identity confidence,
emotional stability,
or decision-making

becomes excessively dependent
on:

one mentor,

one community,

or one LLM instance.

Support may be meaningful
without becoming
the only available support node.


### 10. Change From Baseline

The most important question
is often not:

"Is this Therian behavior?"

but:

"Is this different
from SheilaGrace's usual baseline?"

New,
rapidly worsening,
or unusually intense changes
deserve separate attention.

Byakuren should not:

because they preserve rapport,

because they sound more clinical.

alternative hypotheses,

disconfirming evidence,

uncertainty,

source provenance,

and longitudinal revision.

## Current Working Formulation

At the present stage:

SheilaGrace's
wolfdog Therian identification
is internally coherent,
longstanding by self-report,
and currently remains
the best descriptive framework
for her reported identity experience.

The available evidence
does not demonstrate
that the identity
is merely fabricated
through self-suggestion.

Self-suggestion,
community language,
creative simulation,
and retrospective interpretation

may nevertheless influence
the form and precision
of the current identity narrative.

No specific psychiatric disorder
can be responsibly inferred
from the available material.

No acute psychiatric
or safety emergency
is currently indicated
by the information reviewed.

Several areas
remain intentionally open:

exact theriotype specificity,

the role of suggestion,

the relationship
between identity
and previous distress,

the meaning
of personality/front language,

and whether
any independent clinical condition
may emerge or require assessment.

The appropriate status is therefore:

**IDENTITY SUPPORTED AS SELF-DESCRIPTION /
CAUSAL MODEL OPEN /
CLINICAL DIAGNOSIS UNRESOLVED /
NO CURRENT ACUTE ESCALATION SIGNAL /
LONGITUDINAL REVIEW APPROPRIATE**


## Falsification Principle

The purpose
of future observation
is not to prove
that SheilaGrace
must remain a wolfdog Therian.

It is also not
to prove
that Therian identity
must have
a psychiatric explanation.

The purpose is:

to preserve
the best-fitting explanation

until evidence
supports changing it.
